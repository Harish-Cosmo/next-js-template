# Content and voice

The template ships placeholder copy. This document is the rule for replacing it.

## Voice

- **Direct.** Say the thing. Cut the sentence that clears its throat.
- **Specific.** A number, a name or a constraint beats an adjective. "Ships in 2 days" beats "fast".
- **Calm.** No hype, no exclamation marks, no "revolutionary". Confidence is quiet.
- **Human.** Contractions are fine. Jargon is not, unless the audience uses it.
- **Second person.** "You" for the reader. "We" only when the site is speaking as a team.

## Words to avoid

`leverage`, `seamless`, `cutting-edge`, `game-changing`, `unlock`, `empower`, `solution`, `revolutionize`, `best-in-class`, `world-class`, `synergy`.

If a sentence still works with the word removed, remove it.

## Page anatomy

A page answers three questions in order:

1. **What is this?** The `<h1>` and the first paragraph.
2. **Why should I care?** One concrete benefit or outcome, early.
3. **What do I do next?** One primary action, repeated at the end.

Every section earns its place. If a section can be deleted without losing an answer, delete it.

## Headings

- One `<h1>` per page. It matches the page's promise, not the site name.
- `<h2>` for sections, `<h3>` for subsections. Do not skip a level.
- A heading is a label, not a sentence. Six words is a good ceiling.
- Do not use a heading for visual size. Use a type class.

## Paragraphs

- One idea per paragraph. Three or four sentences is usually enough.
- Front-load the point. The first sentence carries the paragraph.
- Break a long paragraph before it becomes a wall, not after.

## Links

- Anchor text describes the destination: "read the SEO guide", not "click here".
- A link opens in the same tab unless there is a reason. If it opens a new tab, say so.
- Do not link the same phrase twice on one screen.

## Calls to action

- One primary action per screen. A second action is secondary and visually quieter.
- The label is a verb and an object: "Start a project", not "Submit".
- Say what happens next. "We reply within one business day" removes a hesitation.

## Microcopy

- Buttons: verb first, sentence case.
- Errors: what happened, why, and what to do. Never blame the user.
- Empty states: say what will appear here and how to make it appear.
- Labels: one or two words. No colon unless the value follows on the same line.

## Placeholder copy

The home page is a placeholder. When you replace it:

- Rewrite the hero first. It sets the tone for the rest.
- Replace the three "pillars" with the three things that are actually true about the product.
- Replace the "next steps" band with a real call to action, or delete it.
- Remove the line that says the page is a placeholder.
- Delete any section you do not need. An empty section is worse than a short page.

## Editing checklist

- [ ] The `<h1>` states the page's promise.
- [ ] The first paragraph answers "what is this?".
- [ ] One primary action, repeated at the end.
- [ ] No word from the avoid list.
- [ ] Headings do not skip a level.
- [ ] Links have descriptive anchor text.
- [ ] Placeholder copy is gone.
